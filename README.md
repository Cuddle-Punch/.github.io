# Daggerhoarde

**A fast, offline companion for your Daggerheart-compatible tabletop game.**

Daggerhoarde is a single-page app for browsing adversaries and loot at the table — no
sign-in, no server, no tracking. Everything runs in your browser and stays on your
device. Point it at the official reference data, drop in your own homebrew, and have
every stat block, weapon, and item a tap away while you run the game.

**[Open Daggerhoarde](https://daggerhoarde.cuddlepunch.lol/)** — just open the page; it works offline once loaded.

---

## What it does

- **Browse adversaries** — searchable, filterable stat blocks (by tier, type, and biome),
  each with difficulty, thresholds, HP, stress, experiences, attacks, and features.
- **Browse loot** — weapons, armor, consumables, and items, with a built-in loot-roll view.
- **Build encounters** — pull adversaries into an encounter and track the fight.
- **Keep an inventory** — hold onto the gear your party picks up, with currency counters.
- **Create your own** — add custom adversaries and items right in the app, including a
  guided feature picker.
- **Bring your own data** — import any CSV expansion (your homebrew, a campaign set,
  converted content) and it stacks straight into the pool.
- **Download your data** — export any active file back out as a CSV whenever you want.
- **Yours to read** — light and dark themes, pick an accent color, and a layout that
  works on phone and desktop alike.

It's all CSVs under the hood, so the app is as flexible as the data you give it.

---

## Getting data in

Daggerhoarde reads CSV files.
**Settings → Data Management**. Toggle files on and off, download them back out, or
remove them anytime — your data never leaves your device.

## Privacy

There are no accounts, no analytics, and no network calls for your data. Everything you
import, create, or track lives in your own browser's local storage and stays on your
device.
